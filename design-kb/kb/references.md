# References: papers, standards and evidence levels

Source: research task of 2026-11-12 (Crossref records, publisher and index pages). Confidence **High** = bibliographic record confirmed (authors, title, venue, year, DOI). **Medium** = confirmed from an index or secondary page. **UNVERIFIED** = no primary or index record opened; re-check before citing. "Access" is only stated where a free copy was seen.

Evidence-type tags used across the KB: `[experiment]` peer-reviewed study, `[meta-analysis]`, `[industry]` non-peer-reviewed study or report, `[standard]`, `[convention]` practitioner rule with no research base.

## 5. Evidence gaps: present as convention, not research
No peer-reviewed evidence was found for: APCA versus WCAG 2 contrast (an independent write-up says rigorous evaluation is not available and APCA was removed from the WCAG 3 draft in 2023); vestibular-safe UI animation (ground it in WCAG 2.3.3 and `prefers-reduced-motion`); design-system effectiveness; foldable or responsive layout (use platform docs). Tag such claims `[convention]`.
